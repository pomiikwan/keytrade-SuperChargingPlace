公司概况 | Company Overview

五方力量科技有限公司是蓝瞳半导体（深圳）有限公司旗下专注于对外业务拓展的核心子公司， 作为蓝瞳半导体集团的重要对外开放窗口，公司依托集团强大的技术研发实力和产业基础， 专注于三大核心业务领域的市场化运营与全球布局。

Five Force Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter "FIVE FORCE") is the principal subsidiary of Blue Eye Semiconductor (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. ("BES"), specializing in international business expansion. As the primary global business interface of BES Group, FIVE FORCE leverages the Group's robust technical R&D capabilities and industrial infrastructure to focus on market-driven operations and global expansion across three core business segments.
